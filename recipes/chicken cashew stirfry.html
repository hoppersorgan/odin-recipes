Chicken Cashew Stirfry

Home

Ingredients

  • Thin egg noodles
  • Light soy sauce
  • Oyster sauce
  • Chicken
  • White pepper
  • Garlic
  • Onion

Steps

  1. Turn medium sized pan to high heat
  2. Fry onion and garlic for 2 minutes
  3. Add chicken and cook for 5 minutes
  4. Cook noodles as per packet instructions and combine with stifry
